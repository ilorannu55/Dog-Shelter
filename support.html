Dog Shelter Support

Dog Shelter is a free, offline app for small animal shelters. If you need help or want to report a problem, write to info@danglokta.top and we will do our best to reply as soon as we can.

Frequently asked questions

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Everything works offline, and nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

Where is my data stored?
Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes its data, unless you made a backup.

How do I back up my data or move to a new iPhone?
In the app open Settings and choose Export backup. This creates a JSON file that you can save to Files or send to yourself. On the new iPhone choose Restore from backup and pick that file. Restoring replaces all data currently in the app.

How do I delete all my data?
Open Settings and choose Delete all data, or simply delete the app.

Why do I not get reminders?
Turn on Reminders in the app's Settings and allow notifications for Dog Shelter in the iOS Settings app. Reminders are created on your iPhone and cover low supplies, due vaccinations and expiring medicine.

How are the forecasts calculated?
From each animal's feeding amount and medication courses: the stock estimate goes down by itself as days pass. Forecasts are estimates, so recount your stock from time to time with Recount.

Please note

Dog Shelter is a record-keeping tool. It does not give veterinary advice. For your animals' health, please consult a veterinarian.
