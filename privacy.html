Privacy Policy for Dog Shelter

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Dog Shelter ("the app") is published by [[PUBLISHER NAME]] ("we").

Summary

Dog Shelter does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device. We never receive it and cannot access it.

Information you enter

You can enter information about your shelter, animals, supplies and money, for example: the shelter's name and photo, the number of places and the currency; each animal's name, photo, breed, age, weight, microchip number, health records, medications, journal notes and adoption details, including an adopter's name and contact details; supplies, stock levels and prices; donations, income and expenses.

All of it is stored only in the app's local storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you record personal data about other people, such as an adopter's name and contact details, please handle it in line with the laws that apply to you.

Information we collect

None. The app has no account or sign-in, contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and does not connect to the internet or to any server of ours.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose to take a photo of an animal or of your shelter. Photos from your library are chosen through the system photo picker, so the app receives only the photos you select. Photos are stored on your device together with your other data and are never uploaded.

Notifications

When you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device (for low supplies, due vaccinations and expiring medicine). They are created and delivered by your device; no server is involved. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

Backups and sharing

The app can export all your data as a JSON file and restore it from such a file. The file is created on your device and is saved or shared only where you choose, using the standard iOS file and share dialogs. We do not receive it. It contains your data in readable form, so keep it in a safe place. The same applies to an adoption card image: it is created on your device and goes only where you choose to share it.

Third parties

The app does not share data with third parties and does not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services. If you share a file or an image with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

Device backups

Data stored by the app may be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer, according to your device settings. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app stores at any time: open Settings in the app and choose the option to delete all data. Deleting the app from your device also removes all data stored by the app.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
